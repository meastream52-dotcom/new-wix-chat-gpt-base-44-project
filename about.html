About EBook Publishing Profs

We are a passionate team of publishing professionals dedicated to turning your ideas into books that make an impact.

Our Story

From Passion for Books to a Publishing Powerhouse

Founded in 2010, EBook Publishing Profs was born from a simple belief: every person with a story to tell deserves access to professional publishing services. Our founders — a team of authors, editors, and publishing veterans — saw too many aspiring writers give up on their dreams because the process felt too complex or expensive.

We set out to change that. Today, we are a full-service publishing agency with a team of 100+ specialists — ghostwriters, editors, designers, formatters, marketers, and distribution experts — all working under one roof to give your book the best possible chance at success.

Over the past 15+ years, we've published more than 5,000 books across every genre, from business memoirs and self-help guides to romance novels, thrillers, and children's picture books. Our clients have hit bestseller lists on Amazon, landed book deals with traditional publishers, and built thriving author careers.
