General Questions

About Our Services

What publishing services do you offer?

+

We offer a complete range of professional book publishing services including ghostwriting, developmental editing, copy editing, proofreading, book cover design, interior formatting (eBook and print), eBook and print publishing across 40+ platforms, book marketing, children's book publishing, self-publishing consulting, and audiobook production. We can handle your entire project or assist with individual stages.

How long does the publishing process take?

+

Timelines vary by service and project scope. Cover design typically takes 5–10 business days. Formatting takes 3–7 days. Publishing setup takes 3–5 days (plus platform review times). Full ghostwriting projects range from 6–16 weeks depending on length and complexity. We always agree on a timeline before starting, and rush services are available.

How do I get started?

+

Simply fill out our contact form or call us at +1 (800) 555-1234. We'll schedule a free 30-minute consultation to discuss your project, goals, and budget. After the call, we'll provide a detailed proposal and quote within 24 hours. There's no obligation — we just want to understand your project before recommending the right services.

Do you work with first-time authors?

+

Absolutely. Many of our most successful projects have been with first-time authors. We guide you through every step of the process and explain everything clearly. Our self-publishing consulting service is especially popular with first-time authors who want to understand the publishing world while getting expert support.

What genres do you work with?

+

We work with all genres including business, self-help, memoirs, biographies, fiction (literary, thriller, romance, mystery, fantasy, sci-fi), children's books (picture books to YA), health and wellness, spirituality, cookbooks, and more. Our team includes specialists in each major genre category.

Is ghostwriting legal and ethical?

+

Yes, completely. Ghostwriting is a legal, established, and widely practiced profession. Countless published books — including many bestsellers — have been written with the help of ghostwriters. You provide the ideas, expertise, and story; we provide the writing skill. You retain full ownership and authorship rights. We sign strict NDAs on every project.

Who owns the rights to the book?

+

You do — completely. All rights, royalties, and authorship belong to you. We work as a service provider: once your project is complete and paid for, all work product is yours. We have no ongoing claim to your book, your royalties, or your intellectual property.

How do royalties work when you publish my book?

+

We publish your book under your name and your account on each platform. All royalties are paid directly to you by the platforms (Amazon, Apple, Kobo, etc.). We charge only our service fees — we take no percentage of your royalties. You keep 100% of what you earn from your book sales.

Do you offer revisions?

+

Yes. We offer revisions at every stage of the process. For cover design, we include unlimited revisions until you're satisfied. For writing and editing, we include a defined number of revision rounds (specified in your project agreement) with additional rounds available at a small fee. Our goal is for you to love the final product.

Do you offer payment plans?

+

Yes. For larger projects, we typically structure payments in milestones (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on delivery). For multi-service packages, we can also arrange monthly installment plans. Contact us to discuss payment options that work for your budget.

What if I'm not happy with the work?

+

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy, we will make it right — whether that means additional revisions, reassigning to a different team member, or in some cases, a partial refund per our refund policy. We've maintained a 98% client satisfaction rate because we genuinely care about the outcome of every project. Please review our Refund Policy for full details.

Can you publish my book on Amazon only?

+

Yes. If you want to be in Kindle Unlimited (KDP Select), Amazon requires exclusivity for the eBook format. We can publish exclusively to Amazon KDP, or we can take advantage of wide distribution across 40+ platforms if you prefer maximum reach. We'll explain the trade-offs of each approach so you can make the best decision for your book.
