Refund Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

We are committed to your satisfaction. Our refund policy is designed to be fair to both clients and our team of professionals.

1. General Policy

Because our services involve significant creative and professional labor, refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis depending on how much work has been completed at the time of any cancellation or dispute.

2. Cancellation Before Work Begins

If you cancel your project before any work has started, you are entitled to a full refund of any amounts paid, minus any applicable transaction fees.

3. Cancellation After Work Has Begun

  • If work has begun but is less than 25% complete, you will receive a refund of 75% of the project fee paid.
  • If work is 25–50% complete, you will receive a 50% refund.
  • If work is more than 50% complete, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis.

4. Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with the deliverables, we will first work to resolve the issue through revisions at no extra charge. If after reasonable revision attempts the work does not meet the agreed-upon scope, we will discuss an appropriate resolution, which may include a partial or full refund depending on circumstances.

5. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after project completion and full delivery
  • Delays caused by the client (late feedback, missing materials)
  • Services already published or submitted to platforms on the client's behalf

6. How to Request a Refund

Contact us at info@ebookpublishingprofs.com with your project details and reason for the refund request. We will respond within 3 business days.
