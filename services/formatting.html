Formatting

Professional Interior Layouts for Every Platform

Poor formatting breaks the reader's immersion and signals amateur publishing. Our professional typesetters create clean, beautiful book interiors that meet the exact specifications of every major platform and give readers a seamless, enjoyable experience.

Whether you need an eBook that displays perfectly on every device, a print book with professional typography and layout, or both — we deliver files that pass platform quality checks on the first submission.

  • ✓ EPUB 3 and EPUB 2 for all digital platforms
  • ✓ MOBI / KFX for Amazon Kindle
  • ✓ Print-ready PDF for KDP Print and IngramSpark
  • ✓ Table of contents, headers, chapter breaks
  • ✓ Embedded fonts and images handled correctly
  • ✓ Platform-compliant — passes all quality checks
