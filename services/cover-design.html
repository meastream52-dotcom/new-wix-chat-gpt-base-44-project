Cover Design

Covers That Captivate and Convert

Your book cover is your most powerful marketing tool. In a sea of thousands of titles, a professionally designed cover is what makes readers stop scrolling and click. Our award-winning designers create covers that are genre-appropriate, visually striking, and optimized for both digital thumbnails and print.

We handle every detail: front cover, back cover, spine, and full wrap designs — all delivered in the correct specifications for Amazon KDP, IngramSpark, and any other platform you're publishing on.

  • ✓ Custom, original artwork — never templates
  • ✓ Front, back, and spine (full wrap) included
  • ✓ Optimized for eBook thumbnail and print format
  • ✓ KDP Print and IngramSpark specification files
  • ✓ All source files delivered (PSD, AI, PDF)
  • ✓ Multiple design concepts to choose from
  • ✓ Unlimited revisions until you love it
