Our Process

How Our Ghostwriting Process Works

1

Discovery Call

We learn about you, your book idea, target audience, and publishing goals in a free consultation.

2

Outline & Plan

We create a detailed chapter outline and writing plan for your approval before a single word is written.

3

Interviews & Research

In-depth interviews capture your voice, stories, and expertise. We also conduct any necessary research.

4

Writing & Revisions

We write chapter by chapter, sharing drafts as we go. You review and provide feedback at every stage.

5

Final Delivery

A polished, publication-ready manuscript delivered in your preferred format, ready for editing or publishing.
