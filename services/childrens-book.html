Children's Book Publishing Services

From picture books to middle-grade novels — we bring the magic of children's literature to life with professional publishing services.

Children's Publishing

Specialized Publishing for Young Readers

Children's books are a uniquely specialized publishing category. The interplay between text and illustration, age-appropriate reading levels, fixed-layout digital formatting, and the specific requirements of children's book retailers — all demand expertise that goes beyond standard adult publishing.

Our children's book publishing team has produced hundreds of titles across every age category, from board books and picture books to chapter books and YA novels. We coordinate illustrators, handle the complex fixed-layout eBook formatting, and distribute through every major children's channel.

  • ✓ Picture books, early readers, middle grade, YA
  • ✓ Illustrator coordination and management
  • ✓ Fixed-layout eBook formatting for tablets and e-readers
  • ✓ Print book setup (paperback, hardcover, board book)
  • ✓ Age-appropriate text layout and typography
  • ✓ Distribution to Amazon, Apple Books, and school/library networks
