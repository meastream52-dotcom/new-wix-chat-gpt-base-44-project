# Section 3: First 5 Projects

These are chosen to prove the pipeline progressively, build a catalog, test the theater network, and attract increasingly serious talent and capital. Each project is chosen for a strategic reason, not just creative merit.

## Project 1: Animated Short — "The Boundary"

**Months 1-5 | Budget: $40K-$80K | Length: 10-12 minutes**

**What it is:** a stylized animated short about a child in a near-future city who discovers a portal between the physical and digital world. Visually ambitious, emotionally simple, family-friendly.

**Why this is first:**

- Animation is the most forgiving medium for current AI generation (stylized characters tolerate variance)
- No live actors = no SAG complexity on project 1 (voice actors are simpler)
- Proves the full pipeline: script → storyboard → generation → audio → post → distribution
- Family-friendly content is ideal for theater network pilot screenings at churches and schools
- Creates the first IP universe for multi-format expansion and future creator platform

**Strategic outputs:**

- Pipeline proof-of-concept (the 30-second clip at month 3, the full short at month 4)
- The demo you show every investor
- First catalog title for the theater network
- Comic adaptation proving multi-format
- Character LoRAs that become creator platform assets later

**Multi-format expansion:** comic (3-5 pages), 10+ short-form social clips, making-of content, educational "how we made this with AI" content for tech press

## Project 2: Documentary Series — "Building the Studio"

**Months 2-6 | Budget: $20K-$40K | Length: 4 episodes × 15-20 minutes**

**What it is:** a meta-documentary about building your AI studio. Film the process — the tech decisions, the creative struggles, the land development, the first screenings. Brutally honest.

**Why this is second:**

- Documentary requires minimal generative production (talking heads, B-roll, some AI-enhanced visuals)
- Tests the pipeline for non-fiction: interview processing, auto-editing, B-roll generation
- Creates an audience for the studio itself, not just for any one project. This is marketing that's also content.
- Low cost, high narrative value. Investors, press, and talent all watch this.
- Can be released episodically (one episode per month during build phase)

**Strategic outputs:**

- Ongoing content for the platform and theater network while other projects are in production
- Press magnet: tech and entertainment journalists love meta-narratives
- Recruiting tool: potential hires see the culture and vision
- Fundraising asset: "watch episode 3 before our meeting"

**Multi-format expansion:** podcast version (audio strip), blog posts, social clips, behind-the-scenes stills

## Project 3: Live-Action Short — "Meridian"

**Months 3-8 | Budget: $80K-$150K | Length: 15-20 minutes**

**What it is:** a sci-fi thriller about an architect who discovers their AI design tool has been subtly redesigning the city around them. Shot on your 500 acres (exterior locations) with 2-3 shoot days, everything else generative: environments, VFX, backgrounds, score.

**Why this is third:**

- Proves the hybrid model: real actors + AI everything else
- Tests SAG compliance infrastructure in production
- Tests the 500-acre backlot as a production asset
- Sci-fi is the genre where AI VFX is most forgiving and most impressive
- Creates a more "serious" catalog entry for press and talent conversations

**Strategic outputs:**

- The piece that proves "we can make real films, not just animations"
- First SAG project — demonstrates your compliance infrastructure works
- Digital twin of your first backlot location
- Film festival submission (Sundance shorts, SXSW, Tribeca — AI-made films are a press magnet at festivals)

**Multi-format expansion:** novelization (short story), concept art book, making-of documentary episode, comic adaptation, 5+ social clips

## Project 4: Animated Series Pilot — "The Boundary: Chronicles"

**Months 6-10 | Budget: $100K-$200K | Length: 3 episodes × 20 minutes**

**What it is:** expansion of Project 1's universe into a series. New characters, deeper world-building, episodic structure. This is the first test of whether your IP has legs.

**Why this is fourth:**

- Series production tests pipeline at scale (3 episodes, not 1)
- Proves you can maintain character and world consistency across episodes
- Tests the continuity agent seriously for the first time
- Series content is what theater subscribers want: recurring reasons to come back
- Sets up the creator platform: "The Boundary" universe becomes the first sandbox

**Strategic outputs:**

- Recurring content for theater network (weekly screenings)
- IP depth that makes the creator platform viable
- Demonstrates series-level production capability to potential partners and streamers
- First potential licensing opportunity (a streamer might want to pick this up)

**Multi-format expansion:** full comic series (one issue per episode), expanded universe novellas, character-of-the-week social content, interactive episode on creator platform alpha

## Project 5: Community Spotlight — "Our Stories"

**Months 7-11 | Budget: $60K-$100K | Length: 6 short films × 5-10 minutes**

**What it is:** six short films by six different creators from six different communities, all produced using your studio's pipeline with your team as mentors. Source creators from your theater network communities.

**Why this is fifth:**

- Directly validates the creator platform thesis: real people using your tools to tell their stories
- Creates content that resonates in theater network communities (the local church screens a film made by one of its members)
- Generates the best press narrative of any project: "AI studio democratizes filmmaking"
- Tests the creator workflow at a serious level before building the full platform
- Builds community goodwill that protects you from "AI is killing Hollywood" press cycles

**Strategic outputs:**

- Creator platform proof-of-concept with real human stories
- Community engagement content for every theater location
- Evidence for the Anthology Fund and other investors that "democratized creation" is real, not a pitch slide
- Recruiting pipeline: the best community creators might join your studio or become power users on the platform

**Multi-format expansion:** each creator's story gets the full treatment — comic, social clips, podcast interview with the creator, screening at their local theater location
