## Application tips specific to Anthology Fund

- **The application is short. Don't overthink it.** Company name, stage, what you're building, how you use Claude/Anthropic, team, and a link to materials.
- **Include the demo.** Tim Tully has specifically said a video demo of your product is the best way to stand out. Upload your 30-second animated clip or a walkthrough of the pipeline.
- **Emphasize Claude usage.** The fund exists to support the Anthropic ecosystem. Show that Claude is core infrastructure, not a nice-to-have. Your orchestrator running on Claude Code, your development agents using Claude, your distribution agents using Claude — make this concrete.
- **Rolling basis, 2-week response. Apply early.** Don't wait until the round is "ready." They invest pre-seed through Series A with checks starting at $100K.
- **Follow-up partners:** Tim Tully (infrastructure), Deedy Das (technical founders), Amy Wu (consumer). Research which partner's thesis best fits your pitch and request them specifically if the application allows.
- **If you don't hear back in 2 weeks,** email a partner directly with a one-line pitch and your application number. Persistence is expected.

## What happens if all of this works

You've built something that doesn't exist in the world today: a company that creates entertainment at a fraction of traditional cost, distributes it through owned community infrastructure at zero content cost, and opens the tools to anyone who has a story to tell. The studio funds the theater network, the theater network distributes the studio's content, the creator platform feeds new IP back to the studio, and the whole thing runs on an agent stack that gets cheaper and better every quarter.

The last company that controlled production, IP, and exhibition was the pre-1948 Hollywood studio system. It was broken up by antitrust because it was a monopoly over a scarce resource (film distribution). Your version isn't a monopoly — it's abundance. Anyone can create. Anyone can distribute. The theaters are in churches and schools, not on Sunset Boulevard. The economics work because AI made creation cheap and you made distribution communal.

That's the pitch. That's the plan. Now go build it.
